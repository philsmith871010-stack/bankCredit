# What ESMA says about reusing the European Rating Platform

Captured 23 September 2026, from the click-through agreement the ERP shows before it lets a
user search (served by `POST https://registers.esma.europa.eu/publication/agreement` with
`agreement=esma-radar/radar_agreement.html`), and from the registers' general legal notice at
`https://registers.esma.europa.eu/publication/legalNoticePage`.

## The short version

ESMA licenses its own material freely, for commercial or non-commercial use, with attribution.
It grants nothing for the ratings themselves: "Material appearing on the ERP website may be
subject to third party proprietary rights", and "Users should refer to the terms of use of third
parties who provide ESMA with material". The general registers notice, which allows free
reproduction of register information, excludes the ERP by name. So the agencies' own terms
govern what this site may show of their ratings; ESMA steps aside.

Information on ratings available on the European Rating Platform that are issued by CRAs located in the United Kingdom. The ERP includes ratings that are either issued in the European Union, endorsed from a third-country CRAs or issued by third-country certified CRAs, in line with the Credit Rating Agencies Regulation (CRAR). In the ERP, this information is available in the «Rating issuance location» field (located in the «rating details» page under the «rating history» section), which could have the following values: (i) Issued in Union; (ii) Endorsed (iii) Issued by certified CRA. Information on ratings endorsed in the European Union by Kroll Bond Rating Agency Europe Limited. This change will not be reflected immediately in the «Rating issuance location» field in the ERP, which will only be updated by the CRA when reporting a subsequent rating event. Until then, the information displayed in the «Rating issuance location» field will refer to the point in time when the last rating event took place. In this respect, the ratings for which the field «Rating responsible CRA name and LEI» is populated with the entity «Kroll Bond Rating Agency Europe Limited» are to be considered as endorsed in the European Union.
