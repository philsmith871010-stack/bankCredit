How it works

Four passes, and a list that looks after itself.

Every figure comes from the public record or from something you chose. The method is published and applied the same way to every name: no AI, nothing a treasurer cannot follow.

01

The record is readEvery morning

Pillar 3 reports and regulatory returns for the capital and liquidity ratios; the ESMA register for the agencies' ratings; traded prices for the market signal; the day's headlines, judged for what they say about credit.

02

Every name is scoredThe same way, every name

The agency rating is the anchor and the bank's own ratios the pillars, weighed by the published method or by your weightings. A name without a rating or without current capital figures is not scored, and says so.

03

Your list is checkedEach night

Choose the names you accept and say how long you would lend to each. From then on, anything that has moved since you approved a name is flagged on its card.

04

The next name is foundWhenever you like

At each tenor, the names at least as strong as your weakest; any names side by side on any ratio; the whole universe by the average and the weakest of the three main agencies.

What it is worth to an authority

The counterparty review you would do, if you had the week.

Almost none of the work is the judgement. It is finding the filing, reading the ratios out of it, checking the register, and doing it again next quarter for every name. That part is done for you.

01

Every figure sourced and dated

CET1, leverage, liquidity and funding, each with the document, the page, the method and the date it was read from. Nothing is a black box.

02

What changed, on the card

The day you approved each name is the baseline. A downgrade, a ratio that fell, a flagged headline: shown against it, not buried in a feed.

03

The next name, at each tenor

Alternatives at least as strong as your weakest at three months, six, a year: same or better band, ratings and score, and no widening market signal.

04

The three main agencies, on one scale

The average and the weakest of Fitch, S&P and Moody's, with one outlook mark per agency, and eleven years of how they moved.

05

Weightings that are yours

Set how much the rating, the capital, the liquidity and the rest count for, and read every score your way. The published method is always one click back.

06

A list that travels

Your list stays in your browser; a link carries it to a colleague, the committee or your adviser, names and tenors intact.
